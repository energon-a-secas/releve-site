# Releve: product one-pager

**What it is:** Price your Claude Code transcripts at real API rates

**Who it is for:** someone who pays a flat monthly plan for Claude Code, has no
idea whether that is a bargain or a waste, and cannot find out: the plan bills a
subscription and the transcripts hold the tokens, and nothing joins the two. They
are technical enough to run a Python script and skeptical enough to want to see
how every number was derived.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits).

**The scene:** at a desk, at the end of a long stretch of agent work, with a
terminal already open in the next window, wondering what that stretch would have
cost billed by the token.

**What done looks like:** they know their API-equivalent spend, the multiple
against what they actually pay, and the one lever that moves it most, and they
believe the figures because they ran the script themselves and can read the rule
that produced each one.
